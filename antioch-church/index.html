Who We Are

A community built on faith, family and service.

For over four decades, Antioch Community Church has been a home for people from every walk of life — a place to ask honest questions, build real friendships, and grow in faith together. Whether you're visiting for the first time or you've been part of our family for years, there's a seat for you.
