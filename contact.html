Most business websites take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope. Web applications and larger projects are scoped individually after discovery.

Absolutely. We offer ongoing maintenance plans covering updates, backups, security monitoring and small content changes so your site stays healthy after launch.
